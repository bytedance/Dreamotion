Evaluation

Leading human preference.

We evaluate Dreamotion on 674 held-out prompts under a unified human evaluation protocol. Blind pairwise comparisons measure overall user preference. Five-dimensional MOS ratings provide a multidimensional assessment of generation quality, while the overall usability rate separately reflects practical usability.

Human preference ranking

10,507 judgments were collected through blind, side-by-side comparisons and aggregated into an Elo-style global ranking of Dreamotion and recent state-of-the-art text-to-motion models.

Rank-1 with 72% win rate

Multi-dimensional mean opinion score

Five-dimensional MOS evaluates generated motions across five complementary aspects on a 1-to-5 scale. The overall usability rate measures practical usability, representing the proportion of motions that score at least 3 across all five dimensions.

48.6% strictly usable

Data engine

Hierarchical data, targeted growth.

Dreamotion scales data hierarchically and purposefully. Three data tiers play complementary roles: broad coverage builds semantic breadth, targeted refinement repairs capability gaps, and high-fidelity motion capture strengthens fine-grained alignment. The action space is organized by a taxonomy, where recurring model failures guide targeted data expansion.

Method

Structured generation,
progressive learning.

Dreamotion combines structured motion generation with progressive training to support more reliable full-body motion generation.

Model

Root–Body–Hand cascade

Dreamotion generates full-body motion hierarchically from root trajectory to body dynamics and finally hand-level details, in order to preserve fine-grained details while maintaining overall consistency.

Training

Pre-Training → Continued Training → Supervised Fine-Tuning

The training curriculum progresses through three corresponding data tiers, moving from large-scale generalization to targeted refinement and high-fidelity alignment.

Scaling behavior

Scaling data and model capacity.

Automatic evaluation reveals how data and model scaling affect the generation performance. Diversity measures motion variety, DOS P95 captures severe high-frequency artifacts, and R-Precision@3 measures text-motion alignment.

DATA SCALE100K → 3M

Broader data leads to consistent gains in diversity, semantic alignment, and high-frequency motion quality.

MODEL SCALE0.05B → 1B

Larger models improve overall performance up to 0.5B, after which gains largely saturate except for slight improvements in alignment.
